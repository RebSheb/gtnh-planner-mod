Beta 1. Most of the work went into getting the numbers right and making everything faster. Bees are in and a lot of fixes came from your bug reports. It should hold up for real use now. It's still a beta so the planner backs up your plans by itself.

Found a bug? Hit the red bug key in the planner or post in [our thread](https://discord.com/channels/181078474394566657/1531402304530682036) on the GTNH Discord.

## New

**Bees** are modeled now. Frames and upgrades are in and so is jubilant. Add a bee from its NEI page and put it in an Apiary, Magic Apiary or Industrial Apiary.

**Fluid tanks** are modeled now. They turn cells into fluid and fluid back into cells for free. You'll find one under Non-recipe → Other.

**Forced inputs and outputs.** When a plan has to pull something in or push something out it now shows you on the card. Drag it off to make a drawer for it.

<img width="1673" height="481" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/126e1fb4-2371-4ed5-b0ef-7e73973355b0" />

You can also choose where extra output goes or where a missing input comes from. You can even split it between places. Or hit Auto and let the planner pick.

- Inputs that take more than one item get a small orange mark. Scroll on one to swap Coal Dust for Carbon Dust and so on. New cards start on the item you favorited in NEI with Shift + F.
- Share → Share in chat drops your plan in the game chat. Anyone with the planner can click it to open it.
- The tour now goes from NEI all the way to placing machines in your world.

> 📷 **IMAGE 6 – Tour.** A tour note next to what it just did. A step out in the world would be best.

- The planner tells you when an update is out.

## Better

- The planner makes things from raw materials when it can. It only sells what nothing else in the plan can use.
- A machine nothing needs sits at 0 and asks you how many you want.
- Solving is a lot faster. Big plans that took minutes now take about a second.
- Huge plans pan and zoom without lag.
- Edits no longer freeze the game for a moment.
- Scrolling a number updates the plan while you scroll.
- Right-click on the /hr, EU/t and Avg keys goes back a step.
- When you drag a wire every card and port that can take it lights up.

> 📷 **IMAGE 7 – Wire in hand.** Mid-drag. The cards and ports that would take the wire glow green.

- Steam machines show the steam they burn.
- The Dangote Distillus shows its mode. In distillery mode you can set its height.

### In the world

> 📷 **IMAGE 8 – World.** Placed machines with labels on (Shift + I), wire labels and the card over the one you look at (Shift + U).

- Look at a machine and press Shift + U to see its card. Shift + I puts a label on every machine.
- Wire labels are easier to read and don't pile up.
- Machines can be see-through, solid or hidden. All the world keys can be rebound in Controls.
- The settings show a preview of each option.

> 📷 **IMAGE 9 – Settings.** The gear's In the world section with its preview.

## Fixed

- Plans opened from the library came in empty. They have all their cards now, old shares too.
- Changing a card's settings a few times in a row crashed the game. So did deleting a sticky note while typing in it.
- The board came up squashed after adding a recipe from NEI.
- Crop cards were missing drops like Lazulia's Lapis Lazuli. The pin list and the world counted crops instead of farms.
- The Multi Smelter said its coil was too cold.
- An HTGR from NEI showed its whole fuel chain. Now it's the reactor: TRISO fuel in and burned fuel out.
- Pasted plans got the wrong machines and tiers. They also lost their crop farms and generator counts.
- You couldn't stack placed machines on each other.
- Cards stuck out of the round minimap.
- The rate unit and the overview's folded sections reset when you reopened the planner.
- Clicking Avg in the overview folded the machine list.
- The tour changed your GUI scale.
- Crash reports sent from the game were missing the crash. They leave the chat out now too.
- The planner got blamed for NEI's own crashes.

## Install

GTNH 2.9 only: any beta or release candidate. It does not run on 2.8. Stay on 0.2.0-alpha.1 there.

Put `gtnhplanner-0.2.0-beta.1.jar` in your `mods` folder and delete the old one. It's client only. The planner button is at the bottom left of your inventory.

Curious how it works inside? The [technical notes](0.2.0-beta.1-technical.md) go through the solver rewrite, the speed work and every fix from your reports with links into the code.
