Beta 2. You can plan together now. Sign in and your plans save to your account as you edit. Share one and you work on it together live, in game or in a browser.

Found a bug? Hit the red bug key in the planner or post in [our thread](https://discord.com/channels/181078474394566657/1531402304530682036) on the GTNH Discord.

> 📷 **IMAGE 1 – hero.** Two players on one plan. Both cursors with their names.

## New

**Plan together.** Press Invite and pick a player. You see their cursor and their edits as they make them. Undo only takes back your own.

**Your plans in your account.** Sign in and every plan saves as you edit. They open on any computer and in a browser. Signed out nothing changes.

**Task boards.** Pick New task board in the + menu. Tasks hold notes, comments, people and the machines to build. Press a machine's pin key and pick Add to a task. Pick quests straight from your quest book and they tick themselves off when you finish them.

> 📷 **IMAGE 2 – Tasks.** The four columns with a few tasks.

**Groups.** Select cards and press Ctrl+G. Collapse a group to one small card.

- Boxes and arrows on the board. Right-click empty board.
- Buffer drawers sit between the cards that make an item and the ones that use it.
- Drag plan tabs to reorder them.
- Railcraft's Water Tank and boiler are in the non-recipe machines.

## Better

- The top bar is Invite, Share, Plans and Community. The board's keys sit over the board.
- Middle-click a count, a rate or a rule to clear it.
- Right-click a card or a drawer for its menu.
- The tour now shows Invite, Plans and task boards.
- Plans from links and codes keep their layout.
- Machine settings now match the 2.9 RC-2 recipes. The Dangote Distillus height steps from 3 to 12 layers.

## Fixed

- At GTNH's default GUI scale the top bar hid your plan tabs.
- Plans from links and codes lost machine options like coils.
- The Mega Distillation Tower in distillery mode now gets its real speed and parallels, and its height setting works.

## Install

GTNH 2.9 only. Put `gtnhplanner-0.2.0-beta.2.jar` in your `mods` folder and delete the old one. It's client only.
